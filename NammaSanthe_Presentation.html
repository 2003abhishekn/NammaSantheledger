• ABOUT THE DOMAIN

  • The Rural Marketplace: Focusing on "Santhe" (weekly village markets), the economic heart of rural areas.
  • Micro-Vendors: Serving small-scale sellers like vegetable vendors, flower sellers, and snack stalls.
  • Credit-Based Economy: Trust-based "Udari" is essential for rural business but difficult to track.
Page 2

• PROJECT OBJECTIVES

  • Digital Transformation: Transition vendors from paper diaries to a reliable mobile application.
  • Financial Clarity: Provide instant summaries of "Daily Sales" and "Total Pending Dues."
  • Speed & Simplicity: Ensure transaction logging takes less than 5 seconds.
  • Improved Recovery: Professionalize debt collection via WhatsApp reminders.
Page 3

• PROBLEM STATEMENT

  • Memory Dependence: Vendors lose money by forgetting small customer debts.
  • Physical Vulnerability: Pocket diaries get lost, torn, or damaged by rain/dirt.
  • Manual Math: Calculating end-of-day profits and total dues is error-prone and slow.
  • Collection Friction: No easy way to notify customers about outstanding payments.
Page 4

• TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

  • Core: Android Native (Kotlin) for performance and reliability.
  • Database: Room SQLite for secure, offline-first data persistence.
  • Architecture: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) for clean code separation.
  • UI/UX: Material Design 3, custom numeric keypad, and adaptive dashboards.
  • Modern Tools: Kotlin Coroutines, ViewBinding, and KSP for optimized builds.
